# Maître Maher — Modern Arabic & Tunisia Villa 3D Simulator

Interactive AAA WebGL/Three.js 3D Architectural & Interior Simulator for a luxury Modern Arabic & Tunisian Villa (*Sidi Bou Said / Carthage / Hammamet* aesthetic), featuring:
- **Real Poly Haven GLTF 3D Furniture & 1K PBR Materials**
- **3 Time-of-Day Modes (`Sunset`, `Day`, `Night Full LED`)** with 4K GLSL Atmospheric Sky Dome & Poly Haven HDRI PBR Reflections
- **4 Navigation Modes:** `3D Orbit View`, `Top View: 1st Floor (Cutaway)`, `Top View: 2nd Floor (Rooftop Terrace)`, and `Walk Mode (First-Person WASD + Virtual Analog)`
- **Cycles-Style 24-Sample Progressive Photoshoot Renderer**
- **100% Bilingual UI:** English (`EN`) ↔ Tunisian Arabic (`العربية التونسية`)
